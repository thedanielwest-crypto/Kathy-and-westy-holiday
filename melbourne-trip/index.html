Read this bit first

Your trip lands inside AFL Grand Final week. You don't care about the footy, but the city does, and it changes three things.

  • Reverb is closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays. It has been moved to Friday afternoon, which also puts a vinyl exhibition directly before your live music night.
  • Friday 25 Sep is a Victorian public holiday. The Grand Final Parade runs through the city from about 9am. Expect road closures, packed trams and public-holiday surcharges on food.
  • Saturday is Grand Final day. 100,000 people head to the MCG from late morning. Book lunch on the western side of town and leave early for the airport.
