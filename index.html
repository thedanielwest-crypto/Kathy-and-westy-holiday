Three things to know

  • Reverb is closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays. That's why it sits on Thursday with the galleries — it's open 11am to 5pm that day, so it needs to be booked for early afternoon, not late.
  • Friday 25 Sep is a Victorian public holiday — Grand Final eve. The parade shuts down the city from about 9am, which makes a day out in the wine country the smartest thing you could possibly be doing.
  • Saturday is Grand Final day and 100,000 people head to the MCG from late morning. Your flights aren't until the evening, so you've got a full day — just keep it on the western side of town.
